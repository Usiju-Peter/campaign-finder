How this was put together

Every campaign card on Content Rewards Discover (All campaigns) is read every 4 hours, and every campaign with $1,500 or more left was opened to read its platforms, pay per platform, min and max payout and visible rules. Campaigns with less left show only the card data, so their platforms and max payout are blank.

"Left / creator" is budget left divided by the number of creators who have joined. It is a rough measure of competition, not a forecast of earnings. "Spent / day" is how much the campaign paid out over roughly the last 24 hours. "New" means it launched or first appeared in the last 3 days. The casino, regional, on-camera and US-audience flags are keyword guesses from the title, description and visible rules. Most audience rules are hidden until you join, so check them before posting.
