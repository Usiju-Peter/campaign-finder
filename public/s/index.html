Campaign Finder: 11 design passes

Same data and features. Each version is look A (Money desk) improved with one design skill.

  • 01Anti UI Slopready

    One accent, one navy grey family, one radius scale, no decorative gradients, and real loading, empty and error states.

  • 02Impeccableready

    Polish pass: no eyebrow label, a stat strip instead of accent cards, themed selection, scrollbars and focus, menus that open smoothly, and loading, empty and error states.

  • 03Taste Skillready

    Taste pass: real full-colour platform icons, no violet, one motion system (buttons step and press, cards lift, underlines draw, menus drop 6px), a hero entrance on load, plain-spoken headline and buttons.

  • 04Emil Design Engineeringready

    Interaction pass: strong ease-out curves, buttons that press, menus that grow from their trigger and close instantly on Esc, a sliding type switch, crisp tooltip timing, hover only on real pointers.

  • 05Vercel Web Design Guidelinesready

    Guidelines pass: filters live in the URL (share or refresh keeps them), Undo after Clear Filters, skip link, live result count, Title Case, locale-aware money, sortable headers announced, focus never under the bar.

  • 06Frontend Design (Anthropic)ready

    Art-directed pass: a scoreboard typeface, the headline is the live finding in one sentence, picks as a ranked board, no eyebrows, all-caps labels or middle-dot meta, one count-up on load.

  • 07Hallmarkready

    Hallmark redesign as an app screen: every colour and font a token, the ledger leads, figures in one strip, no tracked caps, no dot-joined meta, loading skeleton, empty and error states, filters in the URL with undo.

  • 08Make Interfaces Feel Betterready

    Detail pass: concentric radii, shadows instead of fake borders, 0.96 press, 1.5px icon strokes, 40-44px hit areas, softer menu exits, a cross-fading sort check, balanced headings and tabular numbers.

  • 09UI UX Pro Maxready

    Data-dashboard pass from the UI UX Pro Max database: dense spacing, Fira Sans and Fira Code figures, chart types marked by shape as well as colour, keyboard-operable bars, announced table sorting, 44px touch targets.

  • 10Stageshowready

    Product-demo pass: an animated walkthrough beside the headline. A cursor filters to $5K+, sorts by fewest creators and the best pick rises, with a visible reset; the resting frame shows the result.

  • 11Unslopready

    Unslop pass: a near-neutral ink ramp, one amber accent kept for money and the three best picks, no gradients or glow, sentence-case labels, one number format, a main chart with a quiet ranked list beside it, and a freshness dot as the signature.
